# Testing

Drafts live here until a skill is trusted.

**What goes here:**

- Output from any skill that's new or was just changed.
- Recreations of past months made while building a skill (with `skills/new-skill/`),
  named with " - TEST" at the end.

**When a skill graduates:** after its output has been reviewed against a known-good result
and approved by someone other than the person who ran it. We suggest two clean months. Then
change the skill's "Save to" line from `testing/` to the real close folder, and add a line to
the Changelog in `CLAUDE.md`.

**Housekeeping:** clear out old test files once a skill graduates, so this folder only holds
what's currently being tested.
