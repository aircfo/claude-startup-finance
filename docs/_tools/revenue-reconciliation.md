## What you get

1. **A headline:** matched within $X, or a $Y unexplained gap.
2. **A reconciliation bridge**, from Stripe gross revenue, through each adjustment, to
   QuickBooks recognized revenue.
3. **Open items:** anything unexplained, each with a suggested next step.

## Why the two rarely match on their own

- **Timing.** Stripe is cash in, while your books may recognize revenue over time. Deferred
  revenue is the bridge.
- **Fees.** Stripe nets processing fees out of payouts, but books usually show gross revenue
  and a separate fee expense.
- **Refunds and chargebacks** can land in different periods.
- **Non-revenue Stripe activity**, like transfers, top-ups, and balance adjustments.
- **Revenue outside Stripe**, like ACH, wires, and invoices paid another way.

Your Finance Brain already knows how your revenue maps and books, so the bridge starts from your
mappings instead of rediscovering them each month. It confirms the period and your
recognition method before it starts.

## Try asking

```text
Show me the bridge from Stripe gross revenue to QuickBooks recognized revenue.
```
