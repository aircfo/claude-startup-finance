The business in two sentences

Honestly is per-seat software for finance teams that run on spreadsheets, sold to startups and mid-market companies. Customers pay monthly or annually in advance through Stripe; about 35% of ARR is annual prepaid.

Stage and team

  • Stage and last raise: Series A, $12M, closed December 15, 2025.
  • Headcount: 38. Roster of record is the payroll provider.
  • Finance team: Head of Finance (owns the close and signs off). Outsourced accounting firm (categorization, recs, first-pass JEs). CEO also approves journal entries over $25,000 and bills over $10,000.

Things the books won't tell you

  • Our largest customer always pays its annual invoice about 45 days late. It's their AP cycle, not a collections problem. Don't flag it until day 60.
  • Our cloud credits ran out in August 2026, so the jump in hosting cost in September is expected, not a spike.
  • The November 2026 offsite (about $60,000) is one-time. Exclude it from run-rate burn.
  • Stripe payouts are net of fees. Fees are grossed up through Stripe clearing, so revenue is never understated.
