# Close process

**What this page is for:** when the books are trustworthy, what "closed" means, and who signs
off.

**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-15, by Head of Finance

## Calendar

Business days (BD) after month end.

| BD | Task | Owner | Command |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BD1 | Ramp and bank feeds categorized in QuickBooks | Accounting firm | |
| BD2 | Stripe revenue and deferred revenue entries | Accounting firm | |
| BD3 | Bank and card reconciliations | Accounting firm | `commands/recs/bank-reconciliation.md` |
| BD4 | Accruals and prepaid amortization | Accounting firm | `commands/jes/usage-accrual.md`, `commands/jes/prepaid-amortization.md` |
| BD5 | Payroll and stock comp entries | Accounting firm | |
| BD6 | Flux review | Head of Finance | |
| BD8 | Close sign-off, period locked, reporting package to CEO | Head of Finance | |

## What "closed" means

Every balance-sheet account reconciled, every manual JE approved, clearing accounts at zero,
flux explained, and the period locked in QuickBooks.

Claude treats a month as **open** until all of that is true, and says so when asked about an
open month's numbers.

## Flux review

- **Threshold:** Any P&L line that moves more than $5,000 and 10% month over month.
- **Comparison:** Prior month, and the 2026 Operating Plan v3.
- **Who reviews:** Head of Finance.

## Sign-off

| Step | Who | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Each JE and rec approved | Head of Finance (never the preparer) | Sign-off block on the workpaper |
| Close signed off | Head of Finance | Note in the close checklist |
| Period locked | Head of Finance | QuickBooks closing date set |

## Where the close lives

- **Checklist:** Google Sheets, `Finance/Close/Close Checklist 2026`
- **Workpapers:** Google Drive, `Finance/Close/YYYY-MM/`
- **Reporting package:** Google Slides, sent to the CEO by BD8 and to the board quarterly
